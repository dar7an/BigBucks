How fills work

Orders fill at the last adjusted close in HistoricPriceData. Buys debit cash; sells credit it. A fill is all-or-nothing — if cash or shares are short, nothing posts.

There are no commissions, slippage, or after-hours prints. That is intentional for this course simulator.
